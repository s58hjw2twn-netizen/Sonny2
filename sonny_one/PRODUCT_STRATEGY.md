# Sonny ONE — Product and Research Strategy (2026-10-15)

## Positioning
The personal AI that remembers your world, learns what works for you, and gets better at helping you over time. This is a product ambition, not a validated performance claim.

## Priority sequence
1. Reliable assistant baseline, CI, authenticated tenant isolation, encryption, deletion/export, provenance-aware memory.
2. Verified experience ledger, candidate strategy extraction, champion–challenger held-out evaluation, rollback and independent review.
3. Stable identity and communication adaptation, model router with per-provider privacy disclosures and fallback.
4. Approved proactive project work, personal memory inspection UI, proof-of-learning dashboard.
5. Longitudinal pilot against matched ChatGPT/Claude task workflows, with task completion, correction rate, retention, cost and time saved.

## Separate tracks
Commercial: project copilot first, with measurable personal memory usefulness.
Research: Project Sentience, metacognition, simulated emotions, bounded RSI, and challenger experimentation.
Frozen references: SEB-1.0, CSAT-1.3, RSI v1.3 must remain unchanged.

## Engineering acceptance gates
- CI green, reproducible test environment, threat model and authorization tests.
- Memory provenance, consent, expiry, correction, export and deletion tests; encryption at rest and audit.
- Held-out comparison against fixed champion; independent grading, leakage checks, regression budget, owner sign-off.
- Real-user pilots with informed consent and measurable improvements over baseline.

## Non-claims
No demonstrated consciousness, intrinsic feelings, self-modified model weights, autonomous RSI, or established commercial advantage.
Research prototypes must not be wired into production or given sensitive user data until authenticated and security-reviewed.
