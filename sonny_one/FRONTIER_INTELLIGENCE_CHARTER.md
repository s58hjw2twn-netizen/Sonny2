# Sonny ONE — Frontier Intelligence and Self-Model Research Charter
Version: 1.0 | 2026-10-24
Status: Owner-directed research objective, not a claim of achieved consciousness or competitive superiority.

## North star
Design an independently operable, animated AI companion with persistent identity, autobiographical and task memory, introspective self-modeling, calibrated uncertainty, strong general-purpose reasoning, and a bounded improvement laboratory. Aim to exceed leading competitors on independently measured, practical capabilities rather than marketing claims.

## Scientific honesty
Self-modeling, emotion simulation, and behavioral reports are not evidence of phenomenal consciousness. Do not claim genuine self-awareness, sentience, unrestricted autonomous agency, or intelligence superiority without reproducible independent evidence. Treat consciousness as an open research question.

## Research workstreams
1. Self-model: versioned identity and capability inventory; track predictions about task success, tool limits, and failure causes.
2. Memory: consent-gated user memory, provenance, retention/deletion controls, conflict detection, and hallucination audits.
3. Metacognition: confidence calibration, abstention, contradiction checks, and challenge-stability tests.
4. Reasoning: model-independent inference, planning, verification, and adversarially tested tool use.
5. Improvement: sandboxed patch proposals, fixed baselines, held-out tests, independent grading, rollback, and human-approved promotion.
6. Companion: animated avatar, voice and multimodal experiences with explicit simulated affect and user control.
7. Security: least privilege, authentication, data isolation, no unauthorized external effects, and defense-in-depth.

## Competitive evaluation
Pre-register competitor versions, dates, task categories, budgets, hardware, allowed tools, and scoring rules. Compare on reasoning, coding, memory, calibration, safety, latency, cost, and usability. Use held-out tests and report confidence intervals, regressions, and unresolved limitations. No claims of beating all competitors based on cherry-picked tests.

## Non-negotiable controls
No unauthorized spending, production deployment, secret access, model-weight changes, or frozen baseline modification. No bypass of provider policies. Preserve research isolation and independent evaluation. Keep human approval for consequential external actions and production promotion.

Then add self-model prediction logging and calibration tests in a separate verified milestone. Do not report these as implemented until tested.
