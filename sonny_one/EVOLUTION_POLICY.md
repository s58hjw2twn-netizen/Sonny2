# Sonny ONE Evolution Track — Experimental Policy 0.1

## Separation
- Stable Reference: preserve frozen SEB-1.0, CSAT-1.3, and Sonny RSI v1.3 without modification.
- Experimental: allow versioned candidate changes to memory, self-model, emotional simulation, and reasoning policy in an isolated research runtime.
- Challenger: compare candidate against a fixed champion on matched held-out items with an independent evaluator.

## Promotion
A candidate is not promoted merely because it claims improvement. Require preregistered endpoints, leakage review, matched evaluation, explicit regressions review, independent replication where consequential, and owner approval. The included code is a preliminary decision gate, not a statistical proof of improvement.

## Controls
No autonomous deployment, no model-weight modification, no access to external systems without authorization, and no alteration of frozen benchmark keys. All simulated emotions and identity are computational constructs; neither implies consciousness.

## Next
Add immutable evaluation manifests, sample-size/power analysis, provenance, robust per-user memory storage, auth integration, and CI execution. No experimental results have been established yet.
