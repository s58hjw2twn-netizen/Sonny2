# Sonny Business Assistant — Customer Discovery Script

Purpose: validate the problem and willingness to pay before building integrations. Target owner-operated home-service companies.

## Interview guide (15 minutes)
1. How many new customer inquiries do you receive in a typical week, and through which channels?
2. Walk me through the last inquiry from first contact to quote or booking.
3. How do you track who needs a reply or follow-up?
4. Tell me about a recent inquiry that was delayed, forgotten, or lost. What happened?
5. Roughly how many minutes do you spend preparing replies and quotes each day?
6. Which details must be correct every time (prices, service area, availability, terms)?
7. What software do you already use? What would make switching or adding a tool difficult?
8. If a tool drafted responses but required your approval before sending, what would concern you?
9. What would you need to see to trust it with customer inquiries?
10. If it reliably saved you two hours per week, would $99/month or $199/month be reasonable? Why or why not?
11. Would you try a 14-day pilot using sample or explicitly authorized customer data?
12. What result would make you keep paying after the pilot?

## Interview rules
- Ask about actual recent behavior before pitching the product.
- Do not treat politeness or hypothetical interest as purchase intent.
- Record current workflow, inquiry volume, time spent, failure examples, existing tools, objections, and exact willingness-to-pay response.
- Ask for a concrete next step: pilot date, introduction to decision-maker, or permission to follow up.
- Do not collect customer personal data during discovery.

## Qualification score (0–2 each)
- Pain is frequent and specific.
- Cost of delay/lost inquiries is meaningful.
- Owner has decision authority.
- Workflow can be handled with manual intake and human approval.
- Willingness to pilot is concrete.
- Willingness to pay is stated at a real price point.

Prioritize prospects scoring 8–12/12. If fewer than three qualify after ten interviews, revise the niche or workflow before coding further.
